Folders: pick a level, cull with or without subfolders

The UX spec of FOLDERS-009. The list of Folders becomes a drill-down: a breadcrumb names the level, a row with a › goes one level deeper, and the switch Include subfolders (on by default, remembered per user) decides whether the numbers, the bars and Cull cover the whole subtree. The data is invented; Mark all done and Reopen all change it for real, with Undo in the toast, so you can watch the parent rows follow.

Decisions the mockup shows

  • Drill-down with breadcrumb on every width. A chain of folders without photos of their own and with one subfolder shows as one row (Archive / Analog / Scans 1998); a breadcrumb of more than 4 levels shows the first, “…” and the last two.
  • Include subfolders: on by default, saved per user. Cull opens {paths:[path], subfolders:true} or false.
  • The level card above the rows: Cull this level. A folder with photos of its own and subfolders also gets a grey row Photos directly in ⟨folder⟩, which culls only those.
  • Done stays per folder. A parent row shows a derived line: Done · all n folders, or k of n folders done. Read-only folders count for neither k nor n.
  • Filters Suggested and New photos list the folders below the level flat, one row each with its own photos; All lists the direct subfolders.
  • Folders without photos anywhere below are not shown, and nothing says they are hidden.
  • The folder picker stays for folder sets of several paths.
